# Canonical Field Ownership

Product-level facts include title, description, category, garment type, materials, fiber content, care instructions, country of origin, manufacturer, claims, certifications, product images, and tags.

SKU-level facts include external SKU, color, size, barcode, price, inventory, and SKU images.

Every fact must retain source-document identity, source location, and an evidence span. Conflicting values for fiber content, country of origin, or manufacturer remain candidates until Human Approval resolves them.
