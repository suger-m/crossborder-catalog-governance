# US Apparel Release Gates

- Missing fiber content, country of origin, or care instructions is blocking.
- Missing manufacturer identity requires confirmation unless a versioned rule marks it blocking.
- Conflicting authoritative facts require Human Approval.
- Unsupported regulated, certification, health, antibacterial, organic, or environmental claims require evidence and may block release.
- Marketplace field completeness is reported separately and cannot turn a failed legal check into a pass.
