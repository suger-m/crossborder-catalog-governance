# US apparel compliance

Keep legal conclusions separate from marketplace policies. Use the versioned US compliance taxonomy and preserve official source URLs. Missing fiber content, country of origin, or care instructions block release. Conflicts require Human Approval. Claims such as organic, antibacterial, and environmental benefits require supporting evidence and may block release.
