# eBay US Fashion Policy

Use this Skill only when eBay US is in scope.

## Boundaries

- eBay policy is evaluated independently of US law.
- Missing Item Specifics remain explicit gaps.
- Do not invent Brand, Material, Color, Size, Condition, origin, price, inventory, or images.
- Every variation must retain its canonical SKU identity.
- The first release creates drafts and export files only; it never publishes.
