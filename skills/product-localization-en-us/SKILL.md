# Product Localization for en-US

Translate expression, not facts. Use this Skill before creating Shopify or eBay US drafts when source wording is not already suitable for US commerce.

## Boundaries

- Preserve SKU, fiber percentages, origin, sizes, measurements, care instructions, certifications, and claims exactly.
- Do not strengthen claims, add benefits, or infer missing specifications.
- Use concise US English and the registered category vocabulary.
- If the source is ambiguous, preserve a Listing gap instead of guessing.
- Deterministic channel Tools remain authoritative for final field structure.

Read [references/style-guide.md](references/style-guide.md) for wording and fact-preservation rules.
