# en-US Localization Style

- Prefer short factual titles with garment type and differentiating verified attributes.
- Use plain US English; remove source-language repetition and promotional filler.
- Preserve all numbers, units, percentages, SKU values, sizes, origins, care instructions, and certification names.
- Do not convert measurements unless the system preserves both source and converted values.
- Do not introduce performance, sustainability, health, fit, or quality claims absent from canonical evidence.
