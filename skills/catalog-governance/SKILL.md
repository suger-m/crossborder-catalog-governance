# Catalog governance

Treat Canonical Product as the only fact source. Reject stale Listing drafts and cross-channel differences in SKU, material, origin, size, claims, or dimensions. Pending Human Approval prevents release. A hard legal or policy finding blocks release. Only an approved review may create `listing_package.zip`.
